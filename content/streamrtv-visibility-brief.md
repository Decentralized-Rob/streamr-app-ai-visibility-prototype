# StreamrTV visibility brief

## Short read

Streamr Network has an infrastructure story. StreamrTV / Streamr needs a product discovery layer.

Right now, official Streamr materials explain the product across the main site, blog, roadmap, and product app. The clearest explanations are not concentrated in one simple, crawlable product site for new users.

## Why this matters

If the goal is adoption, people need to find the product before they already know the Streamr ecosystem.

The site should help creators, communities, event hosts, and secure video users understand what the product does, who it is for, and why it is different.

## Proposed experiment

Build a simple prototype site structure using only official Streamr sources.

The prototype should show:

- which pages should exist;
- what search intent each page serves;
- how StreamrTV connects back to Streamr Network;
- where creator/community/secure-video traffic could land;
- how AI systems should understand the product.

## What this is not

- Not a redesign.
- Not product direction.
- Not new claims.
- Not a replacement for the official site.
- Not an SEO audit deck.

## What success looks like

The Streamr team can look at this repo and quickly see a practical way to make StreamrTV easier to find, explain, and promote.
