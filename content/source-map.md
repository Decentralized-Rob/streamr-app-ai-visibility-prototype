## Source-use rules

- If a capability is live, cite the official page/post that says it is live.
- If a capability is on the roadmap, label it as roadmap/future.
- If a capability appears only in code/demo form, label it as demo/prototype unless the team confirms otherwise.
- Do not use social posts, third-party summaries, or community speculation as source-of-truth.

## Working fact highlights

- Streamr Network positions itself as decentralized real-time data and media broadcasting infrastructure.
- StreamrTV was publicly launched in June 2025, according to the Q2 transparency report.
- Official StreamrTV material names one-click streaming, shareable links, screen sharing, ultra-low-latency decentralized broadcasting, and live end-to-end encryption.
- Official roadmap/transparency material shows product work around recordings, chat, payments, many-to-many broadcasting, discoverability/channel features, and plugin capability.
- A later official post shifts the product naming/direction toward Streamr Beta and secure, serverless video meetings.
