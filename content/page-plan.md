# Page plan

## Home

Audience: new users.
Purpose: explain the product and route visitors by use case.
Search role: branded product discovery.

## What is Streamr?

Audience: anyone asking what the product is.
Purpose: canonical definition.
Search role: AI answer page and branded explainer.

## How it works

Audience: users who want the technical difference without developer docs.
Purpose: explain P2P distribution, encryption, and Streamr Network.
Search role: peer-to-peer video, decentralized broadcasting, serverless video.

## For creators

Audience: creators, streamers, broadcasters, podcasters.
Purpose: show practical use cases and avoid overclaiming.
Search role: creator livestreaming, decentralized livestreaming.

## For communities

Audience: Web3 communities, DAOs, founders, event hosts.
Purpose: AMAs, town halls, community calls, demos.
Search role: community livestreaming, AMA streaming, online event streaming.

## For secure meetings

Audience: privacy/security-focused users and teams.
Purpose: reflect official Streamr Beta direction.
Search role: secure video meetings, private video conferencing, serverless video.

## Powered by Streamr Network

Audience: technical users, node/token/community people.
Purpose: connect product back to network infrastructure.
Search role: Streamr Network, DATA, decentralized media infrastructure.

## FAQ

Audience: humans and answer engines.
Purpose: short extracted answers.
Search role: AISEO FAQ layer.
